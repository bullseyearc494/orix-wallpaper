Data management & deletion requests

Orix Wallpaper does not require an Orix account. You can manage device data yourself or contact orix.support@gmail.com about information processed by our services.

Clear your Saved list

Go to Orix Settings → Clear saved wallpapers and confirm. This removes saved hearts on this phone; it does not delete gallery downloads or change Premium.

Remove local app data

Use Android Settings → Apps → Orix Wallpaper → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall Orix. Device menus may differ. This clears preferences, cached media and rewarded unlocks. An Orix live wallpaper requires the app to remain installed.

Delete downloaded images and videos

In your gallery or Files app, remove files in Pictures / Orix Wallpaper and Movies / Orix Wallpaper. Clearing app data does not remove those exported files. A wallpaper already applied through Android may need to be replaced separately.

Crash reports and ad privacy

Turn Crash reports off in Orix Settings; the change applies on the next app run. This does not erase reports already received by Firebase. Use Ad privacy choices when available and Android advertising controls to manage ad-related settings. See our privacy policy for provider details and retention.

Request access or deletion

Email orix.support@gmail.com with subject Orix data request. Describe what you want removed and include only details that help identify the record, such as the email address used for support or a Premium support/order reference. Do not include passwords or card numbers. We may need to verify the request. We will explain if records cannot be identified or must be retained for legal or fraud-prevention reasons. Google-managed data may also require a request to Google.

Cancel subscriptions separately

Deleting data does not cancel renewal or delete your Google Play purchase history. Use Google Play subscriptions to manage or cancel your plan. See subscription terms.
