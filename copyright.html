Copyright & content reports

If you believe a wallpaper violates your copyright or another right, email orix.support@gmail.com with subject Orix content report. We review reports and may restrict or remove content while investigating.

Include enough detail to identify the content

  • The wallpaper title and a screenshot or link identifying it.
  • The original work or a reliable reference to it.
  • Your name and contact details, and whether you own the rights or represent the owner.
  • An explanation of the issue and the action you request.
  • A statement that the information is accurate and that you believe the use is unauthorised; include a signature if required for your formal notice.

Share only information needed to assess the claim. We may contact you for clarification and may share relevant claim details with the content provider when necessary to resolve it, subject to applicable law.

Personal use

Access through Orix does not transfer ownership of artwork, photographs, trademarks or depicted characters. Do not resell or redistribute the catalogue. For other concerns, including inappropriate content, use the same email and identify the wallpaper clearly.
