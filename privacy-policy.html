ORIX WALLPAPER

Your privacy matters.

Privacy policy · Effective 26 October 2026

This policy explains how Orix Wallpaper handles information in the Android app and on this support website. Contact the Orix Wallpaper developer at orix.support@gmail.com for privacy questions.

What the app uses

  • On your phone: saved wallpaper IDs, rewarded-unlock expiry times, preferences, cached media and subscription access state. Orix does not require an Orix account and does not sync favourites to an account.
  • Content delivery: requests to our Cloudflare-hosted services include network information such as an IP address, requested file and request time. This is used to deliver content, maintain service security and prevent abuse.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob serves rewarded and app-open ads. Its SDK processes IP addresses (including approximate location derived from IP), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads and prevent fraud. Processing and personalisation depend on consent, device settings and applicable requirements.
  • Crash diagnostics: Orix uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes and app-not-responding errors. Reports include stack traces, app version, OS/device details and installation identifiers. We do not attach your email or payment token to crash reports. Orix does not include the Firebase Analytics SDK.
  • Wallpaper activity: after a successful Gallery download or wallpaper apply, Orix sends the wallpaper ID, action type, a random installation identifier and a random retry identifier to its Cloudflare-hosted service. The service stores a wallpaper-specific hash of the installation identifier, hashed retry identifiers and aggregate counts so repeated requests are not counted twice and the developer can see downloads, successful applies and distinct installations. Preview views are not counted. No name, email, advertising ID or hardware identifier is attached to these activity reports. Unsent events stay on your phone while offline and are retried later. These records are retained to maintain cumulative statistics and prevent duplicate counting. Counts describe past successful actions, not whether a wallpaper is still in use; reinstalling or clearing app data creates a new installation identifier.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes payments. Orix sends purchase tokens and product information to its verification service to validate Premium status. We do not receive your card number. A support reference can help us locate a subscription issue.
  • Support: we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. A support email prepared by the app includes its version; a problem report also includes the Android version. You can edit the email before sending.

Your controls

In Settings, use Crash reports to change automatic diagnostic reporting. Turning it off takes effect on the next app run. The SDK can store reports locally while reporting is off; Orix requests deletion of unsent reports on disabled app launches. Reports already sent are not removed by this switch.

Use Ad privacy choices when shown to review consent. Android also provides advertising privacy controls. Premium removes ads but does not automatically disable crash reporting. Declining an optional rewarded ad leaves that wallpaper locked; you can keep browsing.

Sharing and service providers

Google (AdMob, Firebase Crashlytics and Google Play), Cloudflare, and our email provider process information to run these features. We do not sell your support correspondence. Advertising SDK processing is described above. Providers may process data outside your country. Read Google’s Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, Google advertising information and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Verified Premium and temporary downloads

A successful Premium verification is encrypted on your phone using Android Keystore. During a temporary connection failure, existing verified access can continue for up to 24 hours after that check and never beyond the verified paid period. New purchases still require server verification. The service receives Google Play subscription notifications to recheck entitlement and acknowledge eligible purchases. A short-lived server cache contains a hash of the purchase token and only the subscription fields needed for access, not the raw token or payment-card details. Media authorization can reuse a verified result for up to 60 seconds. Cache records older than one day and processed-notification identifiers older than 30 days are removed during subsequent billing requests; if no requests occur, cleanup waits until the next request.

Temporary original downloads are managed separately from Gallery files. Use Settings → Clear temporary downloads to remove them. Clearing this cache does not clear your favourites or Gallery downloads.

Storage and retention

Local preferences remain until you clear app data or uninstall. Gallery downloads remain until you delete them in your gallery. Google states that Crashlytics retains crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal. Purchase verification records and support messages are retained only as needed for access validation, support, fraud prevention and legal obligations; provider-held records follow their own policies.

Permissions and security

Orix uses the internet to load content and services, and Android wallpaper APIs to apply wallpapers. Downloads use Android’s media storage. The consumer app does not ask for contacts, microphone or precise location access. Service traffic uses HTTPS. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Requests and deletion

You may request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied, or raise a privacy concern by emailing orix.support@gmail.com. Available rights depend on your location. We may need enough information to identify a record and verify a request. Some records must be retained for legal or fraud-prevention reasons. See Data & deletion for local-data instructions. Clearing data does not cancel a subscription.

Children

Orix is a general-audience wallpaper app, not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact support so we can investigate and address it.

This website and policy updates

These pages use no embedded advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. GitHub Pages may process request information to host and secure the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement. We update this policy when practices change and display the effective date above.
