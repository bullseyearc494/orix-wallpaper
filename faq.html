Help & frequently asked questions

How do I unlock a wallpaper?

Open the wallpaper and choose Watch ad. A completed rewarded ad unlocks that free wallpaper for 24 hours on your phone. Premium-only wallpapers require Premium. Browsing and saving favourites do not require unlocking.

Where are my downloads?

Photos are saved in Pictures / Orix Wallpaper; videos are saved in Movies / Orix Wallpaper. Open your gallery or Files app. Double wallpaper downloads save both home and lock images. Files already downloaded remain after an unlock expires.

How do double wallpapers work?

Preview Home screen and Lock screen to see each image. Choose Set both screens to apply the pair, or Save both to download both images. Android device restrictions can affect wallpaper-setting support.

How do live and 3D wallpapers work?

Live wallpapers play silent looping videos. 3D wallpapers use parallax. Tap the appropriate Set button and confirm in Android’s wallpaper preview. Home/lock choices vary by device. Playback pauses when hidden. Keep Orix installed to keep its live wallpaper.

Why is an ad unavailable?

Check your connection and retry later. Ad supply and consent requirements can affect availability. A reward is required for an ad unlock; a failed or skipped ad does not unlock the wallpaper. Premium provides access without ads.

How do I restore or cancel Premium?

Use Settings → Restore purchases with the same Google Play account. To cancel renewal, choose Manage subscription. Uninstalling the app does not cancel payment. See subscription terms for details.

Do my favourites sync?

Saved wallpapers and ad unlocks are stored on this phone. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local selections and unlocks. Your Google Play subscription can be restored with its purchasing account.

Why do previews load again?

Orix refreshes previews when you return to the app so catalogue changes are visible. Pull down on a gallery to refresh manually. Refreshing requires a network connection.

How do I control crash reporting?

Open Settings → Privacy & diagnostics → Crash reports. Switching it off applies on the next app run. Orix uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes; the privacy policy explains the data involved.

How do I clear data or report a problem?

Settings includes Clear saved wallpapers, Data & deletion and Report a problem. Clearing Saved removes hearts only. Your gallery files and subscription stay unchanged. Contact orix.support@gmail.com if you need help.

Still need help?

Email orix.support@gmail.com. Premium members receive priority support.
