Subscription terms · Effective 22 October 2026

Your membership includes

Every free and Premium wallpaper. No rewarded or app-open ads. Priority email support.

Monthly or yearly

Orix Premium is offered through Google Play as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription. The app displays the price, currency and billing period supplied by Google Play for your account and country. The purchase sheet shows the terms that apply before you confirm. We do not set a single worldwide price on this website.

Renewal and cancellation

Your subscription renews unless cancelled through Google Play. Manage it in Orix Settings → Manage subscription, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Uninstalling Orix, deleting app data or contacting support does not by itself cancel renewal. Google Play shows your renewal date and cancellation outcome. Access normally continues until the paid period ends unless the purchase is refunded, revoked or otherwise no longer entitled.

Restore access

Use the same Google Play account you purchased with, connect to the internet, and choose Settings → Restore purchases. Purchase verification is required; pending payments do not unlock Premium. If restoration fails, contact orix.support@gmail.com with your order reference and a description of the issue. Never send payment-card details.

After successful verification, temporary offline access may continue for up to 24 hours, within the verified paid period. This recovery window does not extend the subscription. New Premium media still needs an online access check. Reconnect and use Restore purchases when asked.

Refunds and payment issues

Payments and refund eligibility are handled under Google Play’s refund policies and applicable law. Contact support for help identifying a transaction; we cannot promise a refund outside those rules.

Priority support and catalogue access

Premium support requests receive priority at orix.support@gmail.com. The app includes a Premium reference in the email draft when access is active. Priority does not guarantee a response time. The catalogue may change. Membership grants personal-use access and does not transfer rights to redistribute wallpapers.

Read the general terms and privacy policy. Manage current subscriptions directly in Google Play.
